KEY CAPABILITIES

Built for Speed, Privacy, and Precision

Reliable scanning and generation tools for business logistics, retail management, and personal productivity.

Instant Camera Scanner

Sub-second camera detection automatically focuses, analyzes, and decodes physical QR codes and barcodes under low-light or angled conditions.

Universal 1D & 2D Barcodes

Full support for Code 128, EAN-13, UPC-A, Code 39, Data Matrix, PDF417, and standard retail and industrial barcode symbologies.

100% Offline Capability

No network connection required. Every barcode generator and scan analysis algorithm executes completely on your device hardware.

Custom Data Generator

Generate customized codes for Wi-Fi access credentials, contact cards (vCard), web URLs, plain text notes, telephone numbers, and email messages.

History & Local Management

Keep track of scanned codes and generated templates in an organized, searchable local history that you can clear or export anytime.

Zero Tracking & Full Privacy

We never collect, store, or transmit your scan history, Wi-Fi credentials, or barcode data to any remote servers. Your privacy is absolute.

APPLICATIONS IN PRACTICE

Essential Utility for Daily Tasks

Practical use cases across work, retail, and everyday home management.

Instant Wi-Fi Connection

Generate QR codes containing home or office Wi-Fi network credentials for guests to connect without typing long complex passwords.

Retail & Product Pricing

Scan standard UPC and EAN retail barcodes to verify product codes, packaging specifications, and SKU numbers.

Digital Business Cards

Create portable vCard QR codes to effortlessly share contact details, phone numbers, and professional website links at events.

Warehouse & Inventory SKU

Generate Code 128 and Code 39 industrial barcodes for tracking packages, asset tags, inventory shelves, and equipment.

PRIVACY & SECURITY

Your Scans and Generated Codes Never Leave Your Device

QR & Barcode Pro operates with strict data isolation. Camera access is strictly utilized for the live optical viewfinder, and barcode generation processes are executed 100% locally on your smartphone processor.

Read Full Privacy Policy
  • Camera Permission: Used solely to optically detect and read barcodes in real-time.
  • Offline Execution: No internet connection is needed or used for decoding.
  • Local History: Your saved codes remain stored locally on your device only.
  • Zero Profiling: No third-party data tracking or behavioral analytics.
