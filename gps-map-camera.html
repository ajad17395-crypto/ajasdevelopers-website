CORE CAPABILITIES

Engineered for Clear Documentation

Everything you need to capture verifiable visual records stamped with high-accuracy spatial and temporal data.

Real-Time GPS Coordinates

Automatically reads device location hardware to imprint accurate latitude, longitude, and elevation onto every frame with sub-second precision.

Reverse-Geocoded Address

Converts raw geographical coordinates into human-readable street names, neighborhood, city, state, and country stamps.

Date & Time Stamping

Imprints exact timestamps in multiple standard formats, providing indisputable temporal evidence for field logs and project verification.

Map Stamp Overlay

Embeds a clear visual mini-map badge directly in the corner of your photo or video so viewers immediately understand the geographical context.

Photo & Video Recording

Seamlessly switch between high-resolution still photography and smooth video recording without losing stamp synchronization or clarity.

Direct Local Storage

Photos and videos remain 100% on your device. We do not upload, analyze, or synchronize your private media to any remote servers.

PRIVACY FIRST

Your Location & Media Stay on Your Device

GPS Map Camera uses your camera and location sensors strictly during active use to generate coordinates and reverse-geocode addresses. We do not track your location in the background, nor do we store or transmit your media to cloud servers.

Read Full Privacy Policy
  • Camera Permission: Used exclusively to preview and capture photos/videos.
  • Location Permission: Used solely in foreground to fetch coordinates for stamp.
  • Local Storage: Captured media is saved directly to your device gallery.
  • Zero Telemetry: No analytics trackers or user profiling.
