Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Android Applications by AJAS Developers

Last Updated: August 08, 2026

Welcome to AJAS Developers ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy when you use our Android mobile applications, including GPS Map Camera Photo & Video, QR & Barcode Pro, and our related software services.

This Privacy Policy details how our applications collect, use, process, and safeguard your data when you install and operate our mobile applications.

1. Information Collection & Use

Our primary principle is to collect only the minimum necessary data required to deliver core application functionality.

  • Location Information (GPS & Geolocation): Applications such as GPS Map Camera Photo & Video require access to your device's fine and coarse location sensors (GPS/Network) solely to overlay geographical stamps (latitude, longitude, altitude, and address) onto the photos and videos you capture. Location data is processed locally on your device in real-time when taking photos and is not stored on or transmitted to external personal servers owned by AJAS Developers.
  • Camera & Media Storage Access: Our camera utilities require access to your device camera to capture images and device storage (Photos/Media) to save the final location-stamped images directly to your device gallery.
  • Non-Personal Device Information: Standard non-personally identifiable diagnostic information (such as Android OS version, device model, screen resolution, and crash log traces) may be processed locally or by standard system libraries to ensure app stability.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Our free Android applications may display third-party advertisements served via Google AdMob or related Google Play Services. These ad networks may use randomized, resetable advertising identifiers (AAID) and technical cookies to deliver relevant ads in accordance with Google Play Developer policies.

3. Device Permissions

When installing or using our apps, you may be prompted to grant runtime device permissions:

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: To obtain accurate coordinates for location stamps.
  • CAMERA: To enable capturing photos within the application interface.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_IMAGES: To save generated photos to your local gallery.

4. Data Security & Local Storage

We prioritize data security. Photos, GPS overlays, and custom settings created within our apps are stored locally on your mobile device. You maintain full ownership and control over all photos and files created using our applications.

5. Children's Privacy Protection

Our applications do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at iajadpatel@gmail.com so we can immediately address the situation.

6. Your Data Rights & Deletion

Because our apps process photo and location data locally on your device without storing personal profiles on cloud databases, you can remove all app data at any time by uninstalling the application or clearing app storage in your Android system settings.

7. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or Android policy guidelines. Any revisions will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
